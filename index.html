Accenture · Engineering Digitisation & PLM · Team lead

Problem

A global heavy-equipment manufacturer was consolidating two sites onto one PLM system. Engineering data held duplicate items and records with more than one owner, and a single error could break design and manufacturing processes downstream.

Approach

Led the migration team. Wrote SQL to extract item and engineering data into CSV, transformed it into import-ready files, and ran item de-duplication and ownership checks before every load. Automated Parallel SME Reviews for a UK Energy Utility

Accenture · Engineering Digitisation & PLM · Business analysis and development
PROFESSIONAL
Requirement → go-liveowned the full lifecycle
15%faster review cycles, with SMEs reviewing in parallel
Allcontractor teams using the process

Problem

In a UK energy utility's Teamcenter review workflow, every step had to be assigned by hand. Subject-matter-expert (SME) reviews ran one after another, so each reviewer had to wait for the previous one to finish, and work stalled whenever someone wasn't available to assign the next task.

Approach

Gathered the requirements directly from the client, then presented the proposed solution to the client: the approach, how it would work, and an impact analysis of how many users would be affected. Once approved, I wrote the user stories in Jira, documented the solution in Confluence, and built custom workflow handlers with the Teamcenter ITK API, including the trigger logic that decides when and to whom reviews are assigned.

Result

Deployed in a planned downtime window agreed with the client. Reviewers are now assigned automatically, SMEs review in parallel instead of waiting in a queue, and the owner is notified automatically, cutting the time spent on assignment and the delays between steps and reducing review cycle times by 15%. The process was rolled out to all of the client's contractor teams, and I worked alongside the other partner teams that used it.

Irish SME Dissolution Risk Prediction

MSc Dissertation · Trinity College Dublin × EY · Team lead (3 people)
EY CAPSTONE
0.94AUC
89.6%of dissolutions in top 20% flagged
14.6months early warning

Problem

Irish insolvencies hit a decade high, and by the time a company's accounts show trouble it's often too late. Could regulatory filing behaviour alone warn us earlier?

Approach

Led the team and built the full codebase: a data pipeline across 814,836 companies and 11 data sources, 84 engineered features, an XGBoost classifier plus an Isolation Forest anomaly layer combined into 4 risk tiers, and SHAP explanations checked against an independent LLM classifier (72% agreement).

Result

AUC 0.94 and average precision 0.63 against a 4% base rate, with a median 14.6-month early warning, beating the 12-month target. Live dashboard presented to around 300 EY attendees and faculty.
